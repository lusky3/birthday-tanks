# 🏍️ Birthday Tanks!

A birthday game for Allan Lusk's 70th birthday (September 22, 2026).

Inspired by Tanks! from Wii Play — fight your way through 70 levels across 7 worlds, each named after a year of Allan's life.

## Play

[Play at: https://lusky3.github.io/birthday-tanks](https://lusky3.github.io/birthday-tanks)

## Controls

**Mobile:** Left virtual joystick to move, tap right side to aim, fire button to shoot, mine button to drop mines.

**Desktop:** WASD/arrows to move, mouse to aim, Space to fire, X to drop mines.

## Worlds

1. 🌟 1956–1965 — The Early Years
2. 🌿 1966–1975 — The Growing Up Years
3. 💧 1976–1985 — The Adventure Years
4. 🧡 1986–1995 — The Family Years
5. 🌲 1996–2005 — The Cottage Years
6. 🛣️ 2006–2015 — The Open Road
7. 🚀 2016–2026 — The Legend Years

## Running Locally

```bash
npx serve . -p 8080
# or
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Open http://localhost:8080
